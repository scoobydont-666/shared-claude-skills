# Action Verb Bank

Start every bullet with a precise, past-tense (or present, for current ongoing) power
verb. Match the verb to the *kind* of impact — don't default to "led" or "managed" for
everything. Vary verbs across a role so bullets don't read as a chant.

## Built / shipped / engineered
Built, Architected, Designed, Engineered, Developed, Shipped, Launched, Delivered,
Implemented, Deployed, Automated, Integrated, Migrated, Rebuilt, Refactored, Productionized,
Prototyped, Scaled, Hardened, Instrumented, Containerized.

## Improved / optimized
Cut, Reduced, Slashed, Accelerated, Optimized, Streamlined, Eliminated, Tuned, Doubled,
Halved, Boosted, Increased, Improved, Stabilized, Modernized, Consolidated, Decoupled,
Simplified, Reclaimed, Recovered.

## Led / owned
Led, Directed, Owned, Drove, Headed, Spearheaded¹, Coordinated, Orchestrated, Managed,
Mentored, Coached, Hired, Scaled (a team), Chaired, Founded, Established, Championed¹.
> ¹ "Spearheaded"/"Championed" are overused AI-tells — use sparingly and only when
> literally true. Prefer "Led", "Drove", "Built".

## Grew / drove business outcomes
Grew, Generated, Drove, Captured, Won, Closed, Saved, Cut (cost), Monetized, Expanded,
Increased (revenue/adoption), Reduced (churn/cost), Negotiated, Forecast, Secured (budget).

## Analyzed / researched
Analyzed, Investigated, Diagnosed, Modeled, Measured, Benchmarked, Evaluated, Audited,
Quantified, Forecasted, Mapped, Root-caused, Profiled, Validated, A/B-tested.

## Created / wrote / communicated
Authored, Wrote, Documented, Defined, Standardized, Established, Specified, Presented,
Briefed, Influenced, Aligned, Advised, Trained, Onboarded, Evangelized.

## Fixed / secured / operated
Resolved, Remediated, Recovered, Secured, Patched, Mitigated, Triaged, Monitored,
Maintained, Operated, Supported, Restored, Debugged, Prevented, Safeguarded.

## Verbs to RETIRE (duty-signaling or weak)
Responsible for · Helped · Worked on · Assisted · Tasked with · Participated in ·
Involved in · Handled · Dealt with · Utilized (→ used) · Successfully (assert nothing —
prove it) · Various · Numerous · Several (→ give the number).

## Quick mapping: what did you actually do?
- Made it faster/cheaper → **Cut, Reduced, Accelerated, Optimized, Saved**
- Made something new → **Built, Designed, Launched, Shipped, Founded**
- Made it bigger → **Scaled, Grew, Expanded, Doubled**
- Made it more reliable/safe → **Hardened, Stabilized, Secured, Remediated**
- Led people/effort → **Led, Drove, Directed, Mentored, Owned**
- Figured something out → **Diagnosed, Root-caused, Analyzed, Modeled**
- Got buy-in / changed minds → **Influenced, Aligned, Championed, Drove adoption**

## Anti-repetition rule
No verb twice in the same role; no more than ~2–3 times in the whole document. If "Led"
opens five bullets, four of them are doing something more specific — name it.
