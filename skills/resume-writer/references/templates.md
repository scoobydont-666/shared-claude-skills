# ATS-Safe Templates

All single-column, standard headers, real text, no tables/columns/graphics. Copy-paste
safe. Render to `.docx` (via `ms-office-suite`) with a standard font (Calibri/Georgia/
Lato 10–12pt) for submission. These are layouts — fill with quantified, tailored content
per the other references.

---

## Template C — Master Resume (the source you tailor from)

Keep ONE long internal doc with **everything** — every role, every accomplishment, every
metric, every tool. Never submitted as-is. Each application = copy → cut to target →
mirror JD keywords → score → ship. Structure it like Template A but exhaustive:

```
NAME · contact

SUMMARY (a few interchangeable variants for different target types)

SKILLS (the full inventory, grouped)

EXPERIENCE
Company — Title · dates
• [every quantified accomplishment you can recall — 8–12 per major role]
  (you'll cut to 4–6 per application)

PROJECTS · EDUCATION · CERTIFICATIONS · PUBLICATIONS · AWARDS (full lists)
```

## Cover letter skeleton (when one is requested/useful)
Short, specific, not a resume rehash:
```
[Hook: why THIS company/role, 1–2 sentences — show you read the posting]
[Proof: 1 paragraph — your single most relevant, quantified win mapped to their need]
[Fit: 1 short paragraph — 2–3 more matches to their must-haves, in their language]
[Close: enthusiasm + clear call to action]
```
Keep it under ~250 words. Mirror the JD; never restate the whole resume.

## LinkedIn "About" (adapted from the summary)
First-person OK here (unlike the resume). 3–5 short paragraphs, keyword-rich for LinkedIn
search, more voice/personality than the resume. Lead with positioning + signature wins;
end with what you're looking for / open to. Keep it consistent with the resume's facts.
